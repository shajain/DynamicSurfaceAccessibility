## 6. Contact classes per chain copy (main pipeline change)
Design (agreed with the user):
- classes `dist4.5`, `salt_bridge`, `hydrogen_bond`, `cation_pi`, `covalent_isopeptide`, `any_bond` (6.5 Å dropped:
  ~90% of copies have an intra-chain atom within 6.5 Å);
- separately for PDB inter-chain, PDB intra-chain (multimer copies) and AlphaFold intra-chain (monomer);
- inter / intra SASA independent of each other; **only the chain copies with the contact** are aggregated;
- no contact = nothing within 4.5 Å in any copy of that source (PDB and AlphaFold separate);
- `purely_intra` = an intra class and no inter-chain bond; peptide aggregation max-of-max, min-of-min, average of
  per-lysine means (over the lysines in the class); no contact requires all lysines;
- B-factors are class independent (plotted per group only);
- never combine PDB and AlphaFold values; plots may mix them.
Implementation: `lysine_properties.py` (`contact_class_properties`, uses the `ProteinContacts` already loaded per
protein), `ms_dsa_annotator.py` (`AVAILABLE_LYSINE_AGGREGATIONS`, `ALL_LYSINE_FLAGS`, `add_purely_intra`),
`build_annotations_v2.py --rebuild`. Rebuild ~13 min; 74 new lysine columns, 61 new peptide columns; all previous
columns identical value for value. Checks: `pdb_inter_any_bond == inter_chain_pdb`; 99.8–100% of contact copies
found in SASA; inter-chain lysines median asa_nz 21.1 Å² over contact copies vs 31.5 Å² over all copies.
Backups of the previous code and tables were kept in the session scratchpad (not in the repo).

## 8. Findings worth remembering
- `asa_nz` > 60 Å²: 16 lysines, artifacts (stretched CE–NZ; 4u7e missing CE, 81.4 Å²).
- No contact never overlaps the distance classes within a source; cation-π (≤ 6 Å) can overlap (10 PDB, 47 AF
  lysines); PDB inter-chain vs AlphaFold no contact overlap is expected.
- Peptides outside all three distance sets are uncovered: no PDB structure (618 peptides), structures without these
  lysines (708), mixed coverage (1, e.g. Q9UNL2 `MAPKGSSKQQSEEDLLLQDFSR`), missing contacts files (3).
- 34.8% of lysine peptides have > 1 K, but only 17.4% (version A) / 13.6% (version B) of shared-charge peptides
  (unverified hypothesis: mixed-label forms of multi-lysine peptides).
- Correlations of MS_DSA with asa_nz and B-factors are weak; structural groups separate clearly in asa_nz but much less
  in MS_DSA.
